about #

I am an AI researcher interested in training useful, superintelligent agents.

Right now I research and post-train computer-use agents at Yutori. We trained Navigator N2, which achieves 65% on OSWorld-V2 and 85% on OSWorld-v1 – comparable to Opus 5 at <10% of the cost per task.

I was our second AI research hire, and have been co-leading rl research/data, leading rl infra, and contributing across the rest of the modeling stack. I also led early product prototyping and the iOS app for Scouts, Yutori’s first consumer product.

Before this, I worked at two other AI startups (Minion, ASAPP), and got started with research as an AI resident at Google, where I researched language model pre-training and fine-tuning.
